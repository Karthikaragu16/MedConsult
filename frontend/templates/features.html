Fever / Viral Flu
Severity: Medium 🟡
✅ Do's
Drink warm fluids • Rest well • Monitor temperature
❌ Don'ts
Avoid cold drinks • No heavy exercise
🏠 Home Remedy
Ginger + Tulsi + Honey tea — 3 times a day
😷
Cough / Common Cold
Severity: Low 🟢
✅ Do's
Steam inhalation • Warm salt water gargle • Stay warm
❌ Don'ts
Avoid cold air • No dairy products
🏠 Home Remedy
Honey + Ginger juice — 1 tsp twice daily
🧠
Headache / Migraine
Severity: Low 🟢
✅ Do's
Rest in dark room • Stay hydrated • Cold compress
❌ Don'ts
Avoid bright screens • Don't skip sleep
🏠 Home Remedy
Peppermint oil on temples and forehead
🤢
Stomach Pain
Severity: Low 🟢
✅ Do's
Eat light meals • Drink warm water • Small portions
❌ Don'ts
Avoid spicy food • No oily snacks
🏠 Home Remedy
Jeera (cumin) water — boil 1 tsp and drink warm
🌸
Skin Rash / Allergy
Severity: Low 🟢
✅ Do's
Use mild soap • Apply aloe vera • Keep skin dry
❌ Don'ts
Don't scratch • Avoid perfumed products
🏠 Home Remedy
Neem leaves paste on affected area for 20 mins
🚨
Chest Pain
Severity: HIGH 🔴 — EMERGENCY
✅ Do's
Sit upright • Call emergency services IMMEDIATELY
❌ Don'ts
Do NOT ignore • Do NOT exercise
⚠️ Warning
Call 108 or visit nearest ER immediately.
